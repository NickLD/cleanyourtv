# Frigate Glance

A community add-on, not made by or affiliated with the Frigate project.

## What it does

While you watch anything on the TV, a small card slides into a corner the moment
[Frigate](https://frigate.video) raises an alert: which camera, what it saw, and the picture. It goes
away on its own a few seconds after the event ends. Two remote buttons, named on the card, dismiss it or
snooze all cards for a while.
